## Apply and recover

Export a fresh backup, restart the local app if needed, and reload online once. The planning refresh applies automatically to an existing browser plan; no seed replacement or manual reimport of personal history is needed. Choose a date in Calendar to see its full roadmap; optional choices/gate confirmations are under “Roadmap choices and demonstrated gates”.

For a code rollback, restore the previous checkout/source while retaining the same browser profile and origin; never clear site data. Normal backup restore/recovery is still available in Settings, and the previous restore point is retained. The planning-only snapshot and revision also retain the previous policy for inspection. Explicitly demonstrated gates and optional choices are included in normal exported backups.
